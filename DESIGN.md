# FlowUPI — Design System & Visual Identity Specification

This document details the visual identity, color palette, design tokens, typography, and component specifications for **FlowUPI**.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity & Visual Positioning

FlowUPI is positioned as a **fast, local-first UPI payment utility**.

- **Aesthetic**: Dark, tactile, high-contrast, compact, and utility-first.
- **Vibe**: Fast, tactile NeoPOP physics, clean fintech precision.
- **Principles**:
  1. Mobile is the primary platform.
  2. High contrast typography with dominant numeric payment amounts.
  3. No generic AI sparkles, trading terminals, or unnecessary neon gradients.

---

## 🖤 Color System & Design Tokens

- **Background (`bg-bg`)**: `#05080E` (Deep near-black)
- **Surface (`bg-bg-surface`)**: `#0B111E` (Dark elevated navy-black)
- **Elevated Element (`bg-bg-elevated`)**: `#121A2D`
- **Primary Brand Accent (`brand-primary`)**: `#00E599` (Electric Emerald)
- **Secondary Accent (`brand-cyan`)**: `#00B8ED` (Clean Cyan)
- **Success (`status-success`)**: `#00E599`
- **Error (`status-error`)**: `#FF4560`
- **Warning (`status-warning`)**: `#FFB800`
- **Border Subtle (`border-border-subtle`)**: `#1E293B`

---

## 📱 Navigation Architecture

- **Desktop**: Clean horizontal navigation bar with `FlowUpiLogo`, primary links, install trigger, and info guide.
- **Mobile**: Minimal header (`FlowUpiLogo` + `SCAN` trigger + Hamburger `☰`), opening a smooth right-side drawer with 48px+ touch targets and `INSTALL FLOWUPI` PWA installation support.
